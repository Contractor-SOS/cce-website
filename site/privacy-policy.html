Privacy Policy

Crown City Exteriors respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including, but not limited to:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Information you provide through website forms, phone calls, emails, or text messages

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Provide estimates and services
  • Communicate about projects, scheduling, and customer support
  • Send service-related text messages when you contact us first

Information sharing

Mobile Opt in, SMS Consent, and phone numbers collected for SMS communication purposes will not be shared with any third party or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Text Messaging

If you text Crown City Exteriors, you agree to receive conversational messages related to your inquiry or project.

  • Message frequency may vary.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page.
