Home Exterior Services: Our Process

  1. Request an Estimate

    It starts with a simple request through our website or a phone call. We’ll ask a few basic questions and schedule a time that works for you.

    No pressure. No sales pitch. Just a conversation.

  2. On-Site Walkthrough

    We meet you at your home for an on-site walkthrough to examine the exterior, review surfaces, and discuss your goals. We look closely at prep needs, problem areas, and any repairs that may be required.

    This step matters. A good paint job starts long before the first brush.

  3. Clear, Detailed Proposal

    You’ll receive a written estimate that clearly outlines:

    • Scope of work
    • Preparation steps
    • Materials being used
    • Timeline expectations

    No vague language. No surprises.

  4. Prep Done the Right Way

    Proper prep is what separates short-term results from long-term ones. Our team takes the time to:

    • Protect landscaping and surfaces
    • Repair minor wood damage as needed
    • Scrape, sand, and prime where required
    • Address problem areas before painting begins

    This is where quality is built.

  5. Professional Application

    Our crews show up on time, respect your property, and work with care. We apply coatings according to manufacturer standards and weather conditions to ensure durability and a clean finish.

    You’ll always know what’s happening and what’s next.

  6. Clean Job Site, Final Walkthrough

    When the work is complete, we clean up thoroughly and walk the project with you to make sure everything meets expectations.

    If something needs attention, we address it before we consider the job finished.

  7. Ongoing Support

    We stand behind our work and are always available if you have questions after the project is complete.

    Our goal isn’t just a finished job. It’s a homeowner who’s confident in the result.
