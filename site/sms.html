Text Message Opt-In

How people opt in to Crown City Exteriors Text Messages, with a screenshot of each opt-in point.

Crown City Exteriors texts only people who opt in. Every opt-in is optional, unchecked by default and never required to get an estimate, buy from us or work with us. Every opt-in gets a confirmation text with HELP and STOP instructions. The program is described in our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

1. Customers: our estimate request form

On our estimate request form, customers enter a mobile number and may check this box:

By checking this box you agree to receive text messages from Crown City Exteriors about your estimate and project (appointment reminders, updates and replies to your requests). Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.

A separate box covers offers:

Yes, send me occasional offers from Crown City Exteriors by text, up to 4 msgs/month. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

2. Offers by keyword: brochures and yard signs

Our brochures and yard signs carry this line. Texting DEALS returns a confirmation text.

Text DEALS to (913) 408-7707 for seasonal offers. Up to 4 msgs/mo. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Terms: crowncityexteriors.com/sms-terms

The confirmation text reads: “Crown City Exteriors: You’re signed up for occasional offers by text. Up to 4 msgs/mo. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.”

3. Subcontractor crews: crew portal profile

Crews opt in on the My Profile tab of their Crown City Exteriors crew portal by checking:

Text me work offers, job schedules and urgent job alerts from Crown City Exteriors. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

4. Employees: staff profile

Employees opt in in their staff profile by checking the same box:

Text me work offers, job schedules and urgent job alerts from Crown City Exteriors. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Texting us first

People who text (913) 408-7707 first get replies to their message only. We don’t add them to any other texts unless they opt in.
