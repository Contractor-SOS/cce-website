Crown City Exteriors Text Messages

These terms cover text messages from Crown City Exteriors (Crown City Exteriors LLC) through our program, Crown City Exteriors Text Messages. By opting in, you confirm that you are the account holder of the mobile number you gave us, or have the account holder’s permission.

What you’ll receive

  • Customer updates: estimate appointment confirmations and reminders, links to view your estimate or proposal, project schedule and status updates, and replies to your questions and requests.
  • Offers (only with separate consent): occasional texts about seasonal offers and services, up to 4 messages a month.
  • Crew and team alerts: work offers, job schedules and reminders, and urgent alerts about active jobs, for subcontractor crews and Crown City Exteriors employees who opt in.

How you opt in

  • Customers check the text-message box on our estimate request form or another of our forms.
  • For offers, check the separate offers box on our forms, or text DEALS to (913) 408-7707.
  • Subcontractor crews opt in in their crew portal profile, and employees in their staff profile.
  • If you text us first, we reply to your message only.

Every opt-in is optional and unchecked by default. Consent is not a condition of purchase. After you opt in, we send one text confirming it, with HELP and STOP instructions. Each opt-in point is shown on our text message opt-in page.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Customer and crew texts depend on your estimate, project or jobs. Offers are limited to 4 messages a month.

Costs

Msg & data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any text from us to stop receiving texts. We also recognize QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE.

Replying to one of our texts with any of these words is Crown City Exteriors’ designated method for opting out of our text messages.

After you opt out, you will receive one text confirming it, and no more texts from any Crown City Exteriors number unless you opt in again. If you ask us to stop in another way, such as by phone or email, we will honor that too. To start receiving texts again, reply START.

Help

Reply HELP to any text from us for help, or contact us:
Phone: (913) 408-7707
Email: sales@crowncityexteriors.com

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not sell your information. Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Our Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information.
