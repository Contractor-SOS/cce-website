Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Crown City Exteriors LLC (“Crown City Exteriors”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you contact us, use the forms on our website or work with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you fill out one of our forms (for example, to request an estimate), or call, email or text us, we collect the information you give us:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • the address of the property where you would like work done
  • details about your project, such as your timeline, the best time to reach you, color preferences and any photos you choose to upload
  • the messages you send us by phone, email or text
  • whether you agreed to receive text messages from us, and separately whether you agreed to receive offers by text, with the date, time and wording you agreed to

If you are a member of a subcontractor crew or a Crown City Exteriors employee, we also keep the contact and work details you give us in your crew portal or staff profile, including whether you agreed to receive work texts.

We also record a few technical details about how you reached a form: the web page you were on, the page that sent you there, campaign tags in the link (for example, from an ad) and your browser type. Our forms keep what you have typed in your own browser for up to 7 days so that a refresh does not erase it; that copy stays on your device.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • respond to your request and prepare your estimate
  • schedule appointments and keep you updated about your project
  • answer your questions and provide customer service
  • send the text messages you agreed to receive (see below)
  • offer and schedule work with our subcontractor crews, and keep our team informed about active jobs
  • keep records of our work and meet our legal, tax and warranty obligations
  • improve our services and understand which of our advertising works

Text messages

Our text-message program is called Crown City Exteriors Text Messages. We text only people who agreed to receive our texts, and agreeing is never a condition of buying from us or working with us.

  • Customer updates: if you check the text-message box on our estimate request form or another of our forms, we text you about your estimate and project: appointment confirmations and reminders, links to view your estimate or proposal, project updates and replies to your requests.
  • Offers: only if you check the separate offers box on our forms, or text DEALS to (913) 408-7707, we may text you occasional offers, up to 4 messages a month.
  • Crew and team alerts: subcontractor crews and employees who opt in in their crew portal or staff profile get work offers, job schedules and urgent alerts about active jobs.
  • If you text us first, we reply to your message. We don’t add you to any other texts unless you opt in.

Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any of our texts to opt out, or HELP for help. Our SMS Terms explain the program in full.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as the companies that host our website and forms, our customer-management software and our text-message and email providers, and only so that they can provide those services to us. We may also share information when the law requires it, to protect our rights or anyone’s safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Sharing with service providers that help us deliver text messages or support our customers is permitted. All other categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes described above: generally while you are a customer or considering our services, plus the time we need for warranty, tax and legal records. After that we delete it or make it anonymous. We keep records of your text-message consent and opt-out requests for as long as we need them to show that we honored your choices.

How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. No way of storing or sending information online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any of our texts to stop all texts from us to that number (we also recognize QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE), or contact us. Reply START to opt back in.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contact us.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time, keeping only the records the law requires us to keep.

Children’s privacy

Our website and services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date shown at the top of this page.
