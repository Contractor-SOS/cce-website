Exterior Painting Services - Got Questions?

Why should I choose Crown City Exteriors over other painters?

Homeowners choose Crown City Exteriors for our attention to preparation, clear communication, clean job sites, and respect for their home. We focus on doing the work right, not rushing through it.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Crown City Exteriors is fully licensed and insured for the work we perform.

How do I get an estimate?

You can request an estimate through our website or by contacting us directly. We’ll schedule a time to walk the exterior of your home, review the scope of work, and talk through your goals. After the walkthrough, you’ll receive a clear, written proposal outlining the work and next steps.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. All estimates are provided at no cost and with no obligation.

How long does an exterior painting project take?

Most exterior projects take several days, depending on the size of the home, surface conditions, and weather. We’ll give you realistic timeline expectations upfront and keep you informed throughout the project.

What kind of preparation do you do before painting?

Preparation is one of the most important parts of the job. Our process may include protecting landscaping, scraping and sanding loose paint, repairing minor wood damage, priming exposed areas, and properly cleaning surfaces before paint is applied.

What type of paint and materials do you use?

We use high-quality, professional-grade materials appropriate for each surface and application. Specific products and coatings will be outlined in your proposal so you know exactly what’s being used on your home.

Will I need to be home during the project?

In most cases, no. As long as we have access to the exterior of the home, our team can complete the work while you go about your day. We’ll communicate clearly before and during the project so you always know what’s happening.

How do you protect my landscaping and property?

We take care to protect plants, walkways, and surrounding surfaces. Drop cloths, coverings, and proper cleanup are part of every job. Respect for your property is a priority.

What if you find issues once the project starts?

If we discover any unexpected conditions such as wood rot or surface damage, we’ll bring it to your attention right away. Any additional work will be discussed and approved before moving forward.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. We stand behind our workmanship. Warranty details are provided in your proposal and reviewed before work begins.

When is payment due?

Payment terms are clearly outlined in your proposal so there are no surprises. We believe in being upfront and transparent.

How far in advance should I schedule?

Exterior projects are often scheduled weeks in advance, especially during peak season. We recommend reaching out as soon as you’re considering a project to secure a spot on the schedule.
